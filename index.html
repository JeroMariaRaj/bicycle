About The Studio

Where Sports Science Meets Cycling Passion

Founded by competitive cyclists and sports biomechanists, VELOMETRIX was built on a single conviction: human anatomy should never be forced to conform to static factory bicycle geometry.

Every millimeter of saddle setback, cleat rotation, and cockpit reach alters muscle activation patterns across your lower back, knees, and feet. Our certified studio operates with clinical precision to eliminate friction, optimize joint angles, and help you ride further and faster with complete comfort.
